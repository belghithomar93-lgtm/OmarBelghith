About

Football
from the
inside.

I don't just talk about sport. I stand in the stadium, ask the questions and bring the story back.

I'm a sports correspondent and football content creator based in Tunisia. As an accredited Diwan Sport correspondent for the Ligue I Tunisie 2025/26 season, I'm on the ground for interviews, reactions and live reports. I've also contributed football coverage for Express FM.

My angle is simple: the game, the people, the fans, the atmosphere. What happens around the pitch matters as much as the score. I followed the Comoros women's national team during their camp in Tunis with ProEvolution Academy (Malta), photographed an A.S. Marsa training session, and organised the football tournament of the JCI World Congress 2025.

Alongside the reporting, I build my own football content for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, and I shoot photos on the same ground. I can cover it, film it, shoot it, edit it and publish it, fast.
